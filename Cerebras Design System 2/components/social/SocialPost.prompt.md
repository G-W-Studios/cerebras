Cerebras campaign / social-ad surface — the signature warm orange-glow gradient with a big **bold uppercase** Manrope headline and the white logo. Use for LinkedIn posts, paid social, airport and web ads.

```jsx
<SocialPost headline="The world's biggest chip" format="square" />
<SocialPost format="story" eyebrow="Now on Cerebras Inference" headline="Experience instant AI" />
<SocialPost format="wide" headline="This is big." image="../../assets/imagery/wafer-planview.png" />
```

Formats: `square` (1:1), `portrait` (4:5), `story` (9:16), `landscape` (16:9), `wide` (1.91:1). Headline auto-scales to the frame. Note: campaign type is Manrope **Bold**, distinct from the website's Manrope Light display.
