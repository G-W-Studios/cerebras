```jsx
<Logo height={32} />                         {/* full lockup, ink */}
<Logo variant="wordmark" height={20} />      {/* wordmark only */}
<Logo height={28} color="var(--white)" />    {/* on dark */}
```

Never recolor outside the brand neutrals + orange. Keep clear space ≈ the symbol's width around the lockup.
