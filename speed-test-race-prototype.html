What changed from the reference

  • One shared header clock (HH:MM:SS) instead of duplicating a timer per panel — matches your frames exactly and is simpler to composite in editing.
  • "Done" badge freezes the exact header time at the moment that model finished, then sits on top of the dimmed panel while the other side keeps generating — no need to tear down or re-animate the finished panel.
  • "FF" chip is a plain text label plus blinking chevrons — reads correctly even muted or on a single frame, which was the original complaint about the old "×5" treatment.
  • Panel cards use a slightly rounded corner (10px) to match your reference rather than the strict 2px web-control radius, since this is a campaign/video asset, not on-site UI — flag if you'd rather keep it sharp.
